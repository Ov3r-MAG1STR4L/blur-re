# railgun.dll settings (read when the game starts).
delay 1.0        # s between the button and a forward shot (Shunt 0.5, Bolt 0.1)
rear_delay 0.5   # backward shot: faster, it is a defensive shot
speed 600        # m/s; range = speed x Perks.Blindfire.LiveTime (10 s)
radius 0.8       # hitbox (swept ray radius); the Bolt has 0.2
height 0.3       # height above the ground
fallrate 2       # how fast it follows the ground downhill
damage 50        # base = one Bolt (50)
falloff 1.5 1.5 1.5   # damage x base for the 1st target, after 1 pierced, after 2+ (user: 1.5 everywhere)
crush 9999       # damage to mines, Shunts, Bolts (orbes) on its line: they are destroyed
spawn 0.35       # chance that a pickup draw may give a railgun (0..1)
test 0           # 1 = TEST: every Bolt the player picks up becomes a railgun (orbs and railgun mixed)
impact_range 300 # m: impact sounds fade out with the distance to your car, silent beyond
fire_range 400   # m: an AI's railgun shot is heard up to this distance
loop_range 400   # m: the flying railgun is heard up to this distance from your car
rear_speed 300   # m/s of a rear shot (600 forward): slower, so it is seen in the mirror
volume 0.7       # master volume of the railgun sounds (Md: lower the base)
duck 0.45        # how much the game (and the music) goes down during the player's charge, like the Shunt (0 = none)
duck_release 1.0 # s to come back after the shot
